Payments and back-office tools for the building-supply counter.

Hillside Payments is a Queens-based payments company for lumber yards, hardware stores and building-supply dealers across New York City.

What we do

Cards at the counter and in the yard

We set stores up to take cards at the register, at the lumber desk, and on deliveries — with the same account and the same reporting behind all three.

Invoices, deposits and house accounts

Estimates that turn into invoices. Deposits on special orders. Partial payments and a balance that settles on delivery. Pay-by-link for contractors who settle up from the truck.

Orders, purchasing and the books

Phone and fax orders entered once. Stock counts that update with every sale. Purchase orders to your suppliers, and a sync to QuickBooks so month-end isn't a project.

Built for the trade

Hardware stores. Lumber yards. Paint, plumbing and electrical supply. Masonry and building-materials dealers.

Stores where half the sales go on a house account, special orders need a deposit, a delivery ticket has to match the invoice, and the counter takes orders by phone all morning. The payment and invoicing tools built for coffee shops don't fit here. Ours are built for this.

About

Hillside Payments was founded in 2026 in Queens, New York, by two people who have spent their careers on opposite sides of the counter: one in payments and commerce platforms, one running building-supply stores in Queens and Brooklyn.

We're starting close to home, with the stores and contractors we already know, and building out from there.
