Payment processing for the register, the table, the front desk and the lumber yard.

Hillside Payments is a Queens-based payments company for independent businesses across New York City: restaurants, shops, clinics, service businesses, and the building-supply trade we know best.

What we do

Card acceptance, wherever you take payment

Countertop terminals, handheld readers for tables and deliveries, online checkout and pay-by-link. One account and one set of reports behind all of it.

Invoices, deposits and balances

Estimates that turn into invoices. Deposits on orders and bookings. Partial payments, recurring billing, and a balance that settles when the work or the order is done.

Orders, inventory and the books

Phone orders entered once. Stock counts that update with every sale. Purchase orders to your suppliers, and a sync to QuickBooks so month-end isn't a project.

Who we work with

Independent businesses in New York City. Each one takes payment a little differently, and the setup should match.

Building supply and hardware
Lumber yards, hardware stores, paint and plumbing supply, building-materials dealers. This is where we started. House accounts, deposits on special orders and delivery tickets that have to match the invoice make it the most demanding setup we do.
Restaurants and cafés
Pay at the counter or at the table, tips and tip reporting, and catering deposits with the balance collected on the day.
Retail shops
The register, an online store on the same catalog, special orders and layaway-style balances, and inventory that stays in sync.
Clinics and medical offices
Co-pays at the front desk, patient balances paid by text or email link, and statements that reconcile cleanly.
Salons, studios and service businesses
Booking deposits, packages and memberships billed on a schedule, and no-show policies you can actually enforce.
Contractors and trades
Estimates that become invoices, progress payments by job, and getting paid from the truck instead of chasing a check.

About

Hillside Payments is family owned and operated in Queens, New York.

We started with the building-supply trade because it's the hardest case: tools that handle a house account and a special-order deposit handle everything else. We're building out from there, close to home, with the businesses we already know.
